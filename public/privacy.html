Your data, explained

Privacy policy.

Effective 20 October 2026

This policy explains how Kod Labs handles information in the CabinView iPhone app, on this website, and when you contact us.

CabinView has no app account, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK. Your website sessions are stored on your iPhone using Apple’s WebKit.

1. Browsing in the app

CabinView does not send your searches, browsing history, website sessions, or website sign-in details to Kod Labs. The app uses Apple’s WebKit to load websites directly. The websites you visit receive your requests, including information such as your IP address and browser details.

Search terms are sent to Google. Websites and search providers may collect information, set cookies, or ask you to sign in under their own privacy policies. See Google’s privacy policy. CabinView does not control those providers’ data practices.

2. Website cookies and storage

WebKit stores website cookies, cache, and other website data on your iPhone so websites can keep sessions between visits. This can include data associated with accounts you choose to sign into on those websites.

To remove it, open More → Clear website data in CabinView and confirm. This signs you out of websites in CabinView and removes its stored website data. It does not clear data in other browsers or delete information held by website providers.

3. Sharing and playback

When you share or copy a link, or select a playback destination, the information needed for that action is handled by the system, destination, or service you choose. Apple, website providers, and connected services handle that activity under their own policies. See Apple’s privacy information.

4. This website

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers Static Assets. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms, or nonessential cookies. Cloudflare processes technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs, and security information, to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may use necessary security technologies. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

5. When you email us

If you contact contact@kodlabs.in, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot issues, and handle your request. Our email service also processes messages to deliver and store them.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, website session data, or other sensitive information. We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the conversation, follow up on an issue, or meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete it, subject to any information we need to retain by law.

6. Your choices and requests

You can clear on-device website data in the app, manage privacy settings with the websites you use, and choose whether to contact us. For access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy requests about information held by Kod Labs, email contact@kodlabs.in. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request.

7. Children’s information

CabinView does not ask children to create an app account or submit personal information. Websites accessed through the app have their own content and privacy practices. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as the app, website, or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kod Labs at contact@kodlabs.in.
