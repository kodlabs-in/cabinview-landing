What do I need to use CabinView?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later and an internet connection. CarPlay features need a compatible vehicle and the appropriate setup.

Can I use my existing website sign-ins?

Websites can keep you signed in using WebKit’s on-device cookies and storage. Some providers restrict sign-in inside embedded browsers, so support varies by website.

Does CabinView play every website’s media?

Website providers decide whether embedded, background, or external playback is supported. Available playback destinations also depend on your connected hardware.

How do I clear my website data?

Open More → Clear website data and confirm. This removes stored website sessions in CabinView and signs you out of those websites. Other browsers are unaffected.
