A little context goes a long way.

For help with a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and CabinView version.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue, if you know them.
  • A website address or screenshot if useful, with personal details removed.

Please leave out passwords, payment details, private browsing content, and website session information.

Before you write

A website won’t load or let me sign in.

Check your connection and try reloading. Some website providers restrict embedded browser sign-in or require an external app. Their sign-in and access requirements are outside CabinView’s control.

Media won’t play on my preferred destination.

Start playback on your iPhone while safely parked, then choose an available system playback destination. Support varies by website and connected hardware; some providers restrict background or external playback.

I want to clear my website sessions.

Open More → Clear website data and confirm. This removes CabinView’s stored website data and signs you out. Other browsers are unaffected.

I have a privacy question.

Read our privacy policy or email contact@kodlabs.in with your question. Please avoid including sensitive information in your initial message.

Prefer a public issue?

You can also report a reproducible bug on CabinView’s GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public, so use email for questions involving personal information.
